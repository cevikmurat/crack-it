CRACK IT!
Classroom Edition
A vocabulary code-cracking race. The teacher hides an English word and gives a clue — students compete to crack it first.

I'm the teacher
Set a secret word + clue, get a room code to give the class, and watch the live leaderboard.
🧑‍🎓
I'm a student
Enter the room code your teacher gives you, then race to crack the word.
How the clues work 💡
🗝️ = a letter that's in the right place  •  🔓 = a correct letter in the wrong place. Every guess must have the same number of letters as the secret word.
How points work ⭐
Be the first to crack the word for a big bonus — but once someone cracks it, the rest of the class has just 35 seconds to get in! 1000 for cracking it · +500 first · podium bonus for the next few (2nd +200 · 3rd +150 · 4th +100) · each 💡 hint costs 400 (reveals a random letter, up to 2). Points add up across words — top the class!
